# CampusEats Brief

## What is CampusEats?

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system that allows students to order food from restaurants on campus. The system connects students, campus restaurants, riders, and the services needed to complete and track a food order.

A student can log in to the system, manage their profile and delivery addresses, browse available restaurants and menus, select food items, add them to a cart, and place an order. The student can then pay for the order online.

After an order is placed and paid for, a rider is assigned to deliver the food. The student can track the delivery and receives notifications as the order moves through different stages, such as order placed, paid, on the way, and delivered.

## Who Uses CampusEats?

The main users and participants in the system are:

- **Students** — browse restaurants and menus, manage their profiles and delivery addresses, add food to their cart, place orders, pay online, and track deliveries.
- **Restaurants** — provide the available food and menu information used by students when ordering.
- **Riders** — are assigned to orders and deliver the food to students.
- **CampusEats System** — manages the overall ordering process, payment flow, delivery tracking, and notifications.

CampusEats is centered around the process of ordering and receiving food on campus. Students choose food from campus restaurants, place and pay for orders, and track their deliveries. Restaurants provide the food and menu information, while riders handle delivery. The system coordinates these activities and keeps students informed through notifications.
